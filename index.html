Jonas Tallberg

Professor of Political Science, Department of Political Science, Stockholm University

I study global governance and European Union politics. My current work examines how domestic politics shapes international cooperation and how artificial intelligence is governed across borders.

My articles have appeared in American Political Science Review, International Organization, International Studies Quarterly, British Journal of Political Science, European Journal of International Relations, Review of International Organizations, European Journal of Political Research, Journal of Common Market Studies and Journal of European Public Policy. My books are published by Cambridge University Press and Oxford University Press.

I received my Ph.D. from Lund University in 1999, am a Wallenberg Scholar, and was elected to the Royal Swedish Academy of Letters, History and Antiquities in 2019.
