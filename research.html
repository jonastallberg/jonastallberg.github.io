Projects

Democracy, Autocracy, and International Cooperation (DEMCOOP)

The project explores why, how, and under what conditions countries’ political regimes affect how they behave in international cooperation. It is funded by the European Research Council, the Swedish Research Council and the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation. I am the principal investigator.

The Global Governance of Artificial Intelligence (GGAI)

Artificial intelligence represents a technological upheaval with the potential to transform human society and is increasingly viewed by states and international organizations as an area of strategic importance. The project is funded by the Marianne and Marcus Wallenberg Foundation.

AI, Power, and Politics

Artificial intelligence is reshaping politics, affecting everything from the circulation of information on media platforms to the ways institutions make decisions. I am the cluster leader of this collaborative research cluster, funded by the Marianne and Marcus Wallenberg Foundation.

Stockholm Center on Global Governance

I am Co-Director of the Stockholm Center on Global Governance, a center of excellence funded by the Swedish Research Council.

Earlier projects

Legitimacy in Global Governance (LegGov), 2016–2021, funded by Riksbankens Jubileumsfond: why, how, and with what consequences do global governance institutions gain, sustain and lose legitimacy?

The Performance of International Organizations, 2014–2019, funded by the Swedish Research Council: how the institutional design of international organizations shapes their performance.
